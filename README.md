## Business Performance Diagnosis Dashboard

# 📊 Project Overview

The Business Performance Diagnosis Dashboard is an interactive data analytics project built using Power BI to evaluate business performance through sales, profit, profit margin, order volume, and sales growth.

The project aims to identify profitable and loss-making product sub-categories, compare regional performance, and uncover opportunities to improve business profitability.

# 🎯 Business Problem

How can a business use sales and profitability data to identify performance issues and make better data-driven decisions?

# 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Track sales growth and profit margin.
- Identify profitable and loss-making sub-categories.
- Compare performance across regions and product categories.
- Support business decisions through interactive visualizations.

# 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI — Interactive dashboard and data visualization
- DAX — Measures and business performance calculations
- Power Query — Data preparation, if used in the workflow
- Microsoft Excel / CSV — Dataset storage and input

# 📈 Dashboard Features

- KPI Cards: Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin %, Sales Growth %, and Total Orders.
- Sales Trend Analysis: Explore sales performance over time.
- Category Analysis: Compare sales across product categories.
- Sub-Category Profitability: Identify high-performing and loss-making sub-categories.
- Regional Analysis: Compare sales and profit across regions.
- Interactive Filters: Filter results by year, region, and category.

# 🚀 Business Insights & Recommendations

1. Key Business Insights

Insight 1: Overall Business Performance
The business generated total sales of 2.30M and total profit of 286.40K, with an overall profit margin of 12.47%. The displayed sales growth is 20.36%, indicating positive growth according to the dashboard's calculation.

Insight 2: Loss-Making Sub-Categories
Tables recorded the largest loss at approximately 18K, followed by Bookcases at 3K and Supplies at 1K. These sub-categories require further investigation to identify the reasons behind their poor profitability.

Insight 3: Top Profit-Contributing Sub-Categories
Copiers generated the highest profit at approximately 56K, followed by Phones at 45K and Accessories at 42K. These sub-categories are important contributors to overall profitability.

Insight 4: Regional Sales Performance
The West region recorded the highest sales at approximately 0.73M, followed by East at 0.68M. The South region had the lowest sales at approximately 0.39M, suggesting an opportunity to investigate its sales performance.

Insight 5: Category Sales Contribution
Technology contributed approximately 36.4% of total sales, followed by Furniture at 32.3% and Office Supplies at 31.3%. Technology was the largest sales-contributing category.

2. Business Recommendations

Recommendation 1: Improve Loss-Making Products
Review the pricing, discount levels, shipping costs and expenses associated with Tables, Bookcases and Supplies to identify opportunities to reduce losses.

Recommendation 2: Strengthen Profitable Sub-Categories
Evaluate opportunities to maintain or increase sales of Copiers, Phones and Accessories while monitoring their profit margins and costs.

Recommendation 3: Investigate Regional Performance
Analyze customer demand, product mix and sales trends in the South region to identify practical opportunities for improving sales and profitability.

3. Conclusion

The analysis indicates that the business generates substantial sales but has opportunities to improve profitability. Addressing loss-making sub-categories, supporting profitable products and investigating regional performance may help management make more informed business decisions.
